Loan Management

Record employee loans; they are deducted automatically when payroll is computed.

Loans are approved by the lender (SSS, Pag-IBIG, a bank, etc.). Payroll deducts them every period from Payment From to Payment To. When an employee's pay isn't enough to cover a loan, it is skipped for that period and flagged, and the loan runs one period longer.

Click an employee to see their loans and add one.
